## Source-grounded boundary

[New Recruit conditions documentation at 4b93391](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/giloushaker/nr-docs/4b93391d049b42f480851af125639cf1ce9d13a3/guide/concepts/conditions.md)
defines candidate collection, all predicates evaluated against the same candidate,
counting passing candidates, and the positional Nth-copy pricing use. The
[editor condition panel at 028526e](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/giloushaker/nr-editor/028526ee2bce36ce26f024e33d762ab9f257445b/components/catalogue/right_panel/fields/Condition.vue)
restricts `before` to local groups. This is public source evidence, not inspection
of the private runtime or proof of arbitrary New Recruit reorder behavior.

Supported: parent-scoped `selections`, repeats=1, nonnegative safe-integer numeric
comparison, one before/self/any/shared/value1 predicate, one to four
instanceOf/notInstanceOf/self/shared/value1 predicates. The original owner is one
selected unit or model occurrence of amount1. Each candidate is tested against
all identity predicates; shared links/categories use the existing identity
evaluator. `before` uses the original owner's durable sibling sequence, preserved
by roster commands/history/storage, never visual sorting or names. It is not a
global same-name index. Child inclusion flags collect their authored candidates;
matching cross-parent descendants have unproven relative positions and remain
unresolved. Unrelated branches and independent forces do not enter the count.
Outer ordinary AND/OR composition remains three-valued with separate completeness.
The existing numeric modifier sequence performs the increment after applicability.

Unsupported: repeats=2, stacked owner or relevant preceding copies, other scopes,
prospective/force owners, nested local predicates, unknown/malformed source
attributes/elements, duplicate containers, ambiguous/missing candidate locations,
and more than 4,096 candidate/identity evaluations per local group. Ordinary
unknown `count` groups remain unresolved. There is no universal local interpreter.
